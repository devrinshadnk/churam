Privacy Policy

soothram.ai Instagram messaging tool · Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy covers the tool that @soothram.ai uses on Instagram to send a game or guide link by direct message when someone comments a keyword (for example "KSRTC") on one of our posts.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and username, as provided by Instagram when you comment on our post or message us.
  • The text of the keyword comment or message that triggered the reply.
  • Whether you follow @soothram.ai, as reported by Instagram, so we can send the link to followers.
  • A record of which messages we sent you, so you do not receive the same message twice.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details.

How we use it

Only to reply to your comment or message with the link you asked for, and to avoid sending duplicates. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private database (Supabase, Mumbai region) that only this tool can access. Records are deleted after 90 days.

Delete your data

Send a direct message to @soothram.ai on Instagram with the word "DELETE". We will remove every record about you within 7 days and confirm by message. Removing our app's access in your Instagram settings also stops any further messages.

Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact: direct message @soothram.ai on Instagram.
